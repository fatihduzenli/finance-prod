## Batch 0 — the bare-substring assertion sweep (branch `step-12g/batch-0`, plan 0.1–0.3)

**Scope (tests and docs only, Fable).** The inventory of 2026-10-28, an AST pass over every `in` /
`not in` comparison inside an `assert` in the 251 files under `backend/tests` (including `e2e/`
and `visual/`), rated each site by the needle and the haystack: HIGH when a needle of up to four
hex or alphanumeric characters, or digits only, is checked against a whole HTML page or another
id-bearing text (an ICS feed, the audit rows' dicts, a `<select>` of UUID option values); MEDIUM
for five to eight characters or a variable needle against a whole page; LOW for punctuated,
`$`-prefixed or long needles, and for JSON, CSV, message or element haystacks.

| | absence (`not in`) | presence (`in`) |
|---|---|---|
| HIGH | 10 (8 real, 2 by rule only: "href", "csrf" on a page with no form) | 7 digit needles against id-bearing HTML, plus 5 four-letter words by rule only |
| MEDIUM | 27 | 30 |
| LOW (left as they are) | about 45 | about 70 |

Every HIGH and MEDIUM site is converted to a whole-token match through the new
`tests/text_helpers.py` (`has_token`, `assert_present`, `assert_absent`, `as_a_token`, `words`):
the needle with no letter or digit on either side, so a hex id or a CSRF token cannot supply it
while the words and numbers a page really shows still match. The two earlier hand-written regexes
(`test_operator_workspace.py`'s "9009") now use the helper too; `test_store_form_fields.py`'s
"tx" fix (13a) already had the same shape. The LOW sites stay: a punctuated needle ("4,321.00",
"2026-13", "dsp-1") or a `$` amount cannot occur inside a token, and JSON, CSV, message and
element haystacks hold no random ids where those checks look.

The HIGH sites, for the record: `test_plan012fa_d_operator.py:295` ("9009" in the store strip,
whose links carry UUIDs), `test_operator_workspace.py:106/111/138/202/231` ("3001" in the store
select's UUID option values; "1001"/"1002" in the header and tab sections), `test_nsf.py:619`
("1234" in the ICS feed), `test_transfer.py:915` ("4111" across every audit row's dicts),
`test_plan012fb_b_adversarial.py:180` ("ZZ" against a page with a CSRF token — the "tx" class),
`test_store_workspace.py:324` ("btn" in a form slice holding the CSRF token),
`test_lease_views.py:331/333` ("6789", "UTC" against the enrol page), `test_statement_links.py:133–135`
