{% comment %} Closes an open bar menu (details.menu, docs/qa/PLAN-012e2-fewer-actions.md D5) on a click outside it and on Escape (focus back on its summary). In an open menu ArrowDown and ArrowUp move between its items (links and buttons alike), Home and End go to the first and the last; Tab leaves the menu as usual (PLAN-015f §3.1). A menu written without the menu roles gets them here (aria-haspopup on the summary, menu on the list, menuitem on each item), since the arrow keys exist only with this script. A convenience only: without it the menu opens and closes with its summary. {% endcomment %}
