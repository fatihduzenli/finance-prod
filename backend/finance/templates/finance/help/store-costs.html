Margin is the operator rent of the lease active today less the landlord rent and the other monthly cost in effect today; it is unknown when any of them is. The P&L shows it.

Months after a closed row have no cost row and read "unknown". To state that the store costs nothing from a date, add a row with landlord rent 0.00: that reads "no cost", a known zero.

The same effective date as the current row corrects it in place. A later date closes the current row the day before and starts a new one, so the history is kept. An earlier date is refused.

A new store's first row starts on its open date.

How the P&L is built. Billed: rent charges by rent month (waived ones left out), setup fees by due date and insurance by due date. Collected: settled payments by settled date, less their settled refunds, plus withheld deposits by resolution date. Landlord rent and other costs are the store's cost row in effect on the first of the month, counted once per store and month. A cost-only row is a month in which the store is held and no lease is active. Credits are money received and not yet applied to a charge: they are held for operators and are not revenue. A credit counts as collected only when it is applied to a charge, in the month applied.
