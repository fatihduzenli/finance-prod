Nothing debits again unless staff reschedule it: Debit again takes the date the operator agreed and makes one invoice for the returned amount plus the NSF fee (one amount on an operator's first return, another afterwards), debited from the linked bank account on that date, or sent by e-mail when the operator has none.

Repeating debits. A repeating debit (New invoice, How to collect: "Set up autopay") repeats one item as bank debits, monthly, weekly or yearly, ending after a number of payments, on a date, or never. Each debit is its own charge, created by the daily run a week before its date and debited once from the linked bank account through {{ company.provider_name }}. Rent is never repeated this way: the lease's own autopay collects it. On the store's Autopay tab, billing pauses a plan (the debit not yet made is cancelled) and resumes it (from its next date after today); an admin stops it for good or changes its end.
