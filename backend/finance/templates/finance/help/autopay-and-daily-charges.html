Creating charges also catches up: every debit date from a lease's start to today that has no charge gets one, due on that date (or on the start date when the first month's debit day is before it), the first month prorated. It looks back at most {{ catch_up_months }} months, this month included.

"Update from {{ company.provider_name }}" reads {{ company.provider_name }}'s autopay invoices only. An invoice made on the {{ company.provider_name }} dashboard is not matched here: attach it from its charge's page with "Attach {{ company.provider_name }} invoice".

"Large debit" marks an amount above {{ large_debit_threshold|money }}. That amount is a placeholder until {{ company.provider_name }} states its per-transaction limit.

A returned debit adds the NSF fee as its own charge at once. One returned for insufficient or uncollected funds (R01, R09) is debited again automatically, for the returned amount only, on the third business day, unless staff press Stop automatic retry or log a call, e-mail, visit or promise for the lease within two business days; at most twice within 180 days. Any other return is not retried and its lease is marked Needs review. For those, and for a stopped retry, Debit again takes the date the operator agreed and debits the returned amount from the linked bank account on that date, or sends it by e-mail when the operator has none.

Repeating debits. A repeating debit (New invoice, How to collect: "Set up autopay") repeats one item as bank debits, monthly, weekly or yearly, ending after a number of payments, on a date, or never. Each debit is its own charge, created by the daily run a week before its date and debited once from the linked bank account through {{ company.provider_name }}. Rent is never repeated this way: the lease's own autopay collects it. On the store's Autopay tab, billing pauses a plan (the debit not yet made is cancelled) and resumes it (from its next date after today); an admin stops it for good or changes its end.
