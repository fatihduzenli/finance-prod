Reference

A Zelle confirmation, a wire reference or a check number; never an account number. Reference is optional. When the payment marks an invoice paid in {{ company.provider_name }}, its letters, digits and hyphens (up to 25) are sent there with it.

A payment received outside {{ company.provider_name }}

Add payment › "Record a payment received outside {{ company.provider_name }}" records a Zelle, wire, check or cash payment already received: the method, the amount, the day it was received and a note saying what was received. Nothing is debited.

When the charge has an invoice in {{ company.provider_name }} and the payment covers its whole price, that invoice is then marked paid in {{ company.provider_name }}: {{ company.provider_name }} shows it paid and sends no reminder. The money itself never went through {{ company.provider_name }}.

A partial payment

A payment of part of the amount is recorded here only: the {{ company.provider_name }} invoice is not marked paid, and it stays open until the rest is paid. The charge reads Partially paid with the balance still owed.

Voiding a payment recorded here

On the payment's page, More ▾ › Void payment voids a settled Zelle, wire, check, cash or other payment on a rent or one-off charge. It needs the billing role or above and a reason (at most 200 characters, never a card or account number). The payment is not deleted: it stays, marked Voided, with who voided it, when and why. Its amount is owed on the charge again, so a paid charge reads Partially paid, or owed in full when it was its only payment. Nothing is sent to {{ company.provider_name }}.

If the payment had marked a {{ company.provider_name }} invoice paid, that invoice stays marked paid there: the app cannot unmark it. Unmark it in the {{ company.provider_name }} dashboard; until it shows open again, the charge is listed under Needs review. If an installment plan was made after the payment, the voided amount goes back to the charge, not to the plan.

Refunds and voids of a {{ company.provider_name }} payment

A bank debit made through {{ company.provider_name }} is refunded or voided by an admin, from its payment's page. Refund sends part or all of a settled payment back to the operator through {{ company.provider_name }}. Void cancels the whole payment in {{ company.provider_name }} before it moves money; a payment that has refunds cannot be voided. Neither can be undone here.

Splitting a charge into installments

Split into installments, on a charge's page, splits what is still owed on the charge into equal installments, the last one carrying the rounding cent. Each installment is a charge of its own, collected the way this charge is; while the plan runs, this charge is paid through them.
