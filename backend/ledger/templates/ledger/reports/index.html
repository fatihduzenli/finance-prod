Reports

Every report reads stored exact amounts in US dollars and exports CSV with the rows it shows.

  • A/R aging: open balances by operator and days past due, as of today. Its total is the dashboard's total outstanding.
  • Revenue by month: billed and collected, rent, insurance and other fees; collected by settled month.
  • Payments by method: settled payments per month by ACH, Zelle, wire and other.
  • Deposits versus transactions: bank deposits against settled ACH payments, with unmatched items.
  • Profit and loss: by store, operator or month, with store costs and margins.
  • Customer statement: open an operator from the operator list and choose Statement.
