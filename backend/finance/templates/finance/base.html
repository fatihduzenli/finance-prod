{% comment %} Layout of the staff screens (docs/qa/PLAN-006-operators.md; the shell of docs/qa/PLAN-012c-design-pass.md D3). Self-contained: no external asset; the style sheets (tokens.css, then app.css), the manifest, the icons and the service worker are the app's own (PLAN-011 D9, PLAN-012c D4). The navy top bar carries the logo (company.logo_url: the brand's dark logo, or a stored document the company settings row names) with the company name as its text alternative, the company name as text only beside an override logo (the brand logo carries the wordmark), the product name, the search box (PLAN-012d D17, a GET form to ledger:search) and the user menu (Settings, Sign out). The left navigation (900 px and wider) lists Dashboard, Stores, Operators, Monthly rent overview, Reports and Admin with their pages, each page only for the roles that may open it (Disputes, Daily charges and Nightly check billing and admin; Activity log and System settings admin; Messages from the processor every role, as its list is read-only); under 900 px it folds behind "Menu", a checkbox and its label: no script. A page that needs the whole width (the grid: twelve months at 1,280 px, PLAN-011b D2) sets the shell_class block to " shell-folded" and keeps the folded menu at every width. The current page's link carries aria-current="page" and its group's heading aria-current="true" (finance.context_processors.navigation). The tab title is " · " (finance.context_processors.company): each page's title block holds the page part only. Flash messages are .toast items (PLAN-012c D5): a dismiss button (the one script below removes the message), a CSS fade (opacity) after 8 s for success and information, the text kept in the page. print.css (media="print") drops the bar, the navigation and the messages and shows the print header (the company name and the date) and footer ("Printed ") (D6). The SANDBOX banner and the title prefix are injected by finance.middleware.SandboxBannerMiddleware.
