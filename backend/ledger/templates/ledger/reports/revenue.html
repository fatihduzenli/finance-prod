Revenue is by settled month: collected amounts count in the month their payment settled, less refunds settled that month. Unpaid by age is by due date. Billed: rent by rent month (waived charges left out), insurance and other fees by due date (waived and voided charges left out). Deposits held are not revenue and are not here; the part of a deposit withheld at the end of a lease counts as other fees collected in the month it was withheld. Amounts in US dollars.
