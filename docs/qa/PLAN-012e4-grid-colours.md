# PLAN-012e-4 — Follow-up F3: the Monthly rent overview's colours, blank cells and store column

**Owner's text:** 2026-10-22 ("Follow-up F3 — Monthly rent overview, with the CI changes in the
next push"). **Author:** Fable. **Implementation:** Opus, one batch. **Status:** Phase A plan.

## 1. Restated objective

The grid's cells read by colour family and still by text and glyph: Paid green (as now); Unpaid
red, light red while the charge is not yet overdue (today's status `due`) and dark red with
white text once overdue (`unpaid`), so the legend's "Due" item goes and "Unpaid" shows its two
shades; Pending yellow; Partly paid light orange; Waived grey. The same mapping reaches the
status badges everywhere (the P&L, the dashboard, the charge tables). A month before the lease
started or after it ended is a blank cell; "Not yet due" is only a future month inside the lease.
The store column reads "#1991 Istanbul" on its first line (number, then name, one link) and the
operator's name on its second; the city and the channel leave the cell (the channel stays a
filter and a column of the download). Baselines regenerated; tests for the mapping and the
blank cells.

## 2. Affected packages

`backend/finance/static/finance/tokens.css` (the status tokens and their documented pairs),
`app.css` (the badges), `backend/ledger/services/grid.py` (the cell rule for months outside the
lease; the cell text of `due`), `ledger/templates/ledger/grid/page.html` (the cell styles, the
legend, the store cell), the components tag (the badge for `due`), `backend/tests`, `docs/`.
Stored values unchanged (`RentChargeStatus.DUE` stays; only its colour and text change).

## 3. Decisions

- **D1. Tokens.** `--status-paid-*` unchanged (green). `--status-unpaid-bg` becomes a dark red
  with `--status-unpaid-ink: #FFFFFF` (AA ≥ 4.5), the inset border dropped; a new pair
  `--status-unpaid-soft-bg` (light red) with `--status-unpaid-soft-ink` (a dark red ink, AA) for
  the not-yet-overdue state; `--status-pending-*` yellow (a light yellow fill with a dark ink,
  AA); `--status-partial-*` light orange (as today, checked); `--status-waived-*` grey (as today).
  `--status-due-*` is removed and every use points at the soft unpaid pair. Every pair line in
  `tokens.css` updated with its measured ratio; the contrast test of PLAN-012c Batch E holds.
- **D2. Cells and text.** `.cell-due` takes the soft pair, `.cell-unpaid` the dark pair; the cell
  text for `due` reads "Unpaid $X" like `unpaid` (the aria-label too), with the glyphs kept
  (`○` not yet overdue, `◻` overdue) so colour is never the only signal; the legend lists
  "Unpaid, not yet overdue" (soft) and "Unpaid, overdue" (dark) in place of "Due" and "Unpaid",
  then Pending, Partly paid, Waived, Paid in the order the owner wrote them (Paid first). The
  badge tag maps `due` to a `badge-due` that uses the soft pair with the "Unpaid" wording kept
  as the label the charge pages already show ("Due" as a charge status label stays a stored
  label question for Batch D of 12c: unchanged here); `badge-unpaid` dark with white text;
  `badge-pending` yellow; `badge-partial` light orange; `badge-waived` grey.
- **D3. Blank cells.** In `_cell`, a month with no charge is blank when it is before the month
  of the lease's start or after the month of the lease's end (the row's first lease start and
  last lease end, the same leases the row already uses); "Not yet due" only when the month is
  after today's month and inside a lease; a month inside the lease with no charge and not in
  the future stays blank as today. The aria-label of a blank cell names the month only.
- **D4. Store column.** First line: `<a>` "#1991 Istanbul" (the number with "#", a space, the
  name; a store without a number shows the name only); second line: the operator's display name
  ("No operator" when none), muted. The city and the channel leave the cell; the `q` filter still
  matches the city; the channel filter and the CSV's channel column are unchanged. The column's
  220 px width stays (PLAN-011b D2: twelve months at 1,280 px).
- **D5. Baselines.** The grid pages (1280, 390) and any page whose badges change colour
  (dashboard, store tabs, operator, P&L if badges show) are regenerated by Fable in the
  Playwright image.

## 4. Test plan

Opus: the cell class per status and the text "Unpaid" for `due` and `unpaid` with their glyphs;
the legend's items and order; a month before the lease start, after the lease end, inside the
lease in the future, inside the lease in the past with no charge (blank), each with its text and
aria-label; the store cell's two lines with and without a number; the CSV channel column
unchanged; the contrast pairs documented (the existing test), the badges' tokens. Fable:
adversarial: a store name with markup in the store cell; an overdue boundary (a charge due today
is `due`, not overdue; due yesterday is `unpaid`) shown in the right shade; a lease that starts
mid-window and one that ended mid-window produce blank cells outside and no "Not yet due"
outside; two leases on one store (a transfer) leave no blank gap between them in the month of
the hand-over; the dashboard's and the P&L's badges carry the same token names as the grid's
cells (a CSS test); the legend has no "Due" item.

## 5. Risk register

A token change reaches every badge: the contrast test and the visual suite catch a drift; the
blank-cell rule must not hide a charge (a cell with a charge is never blank: pinned); the store
cell's width unchanged.

## 6. Acceptance

On staging: the grid reads by the five colour families with the two unpaid shades; blank cells
outside the lease; "#1991 Istanbul" and the operator in the store column; the visual job green.
