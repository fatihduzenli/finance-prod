Lines

Item: choose Rent, an existing open charge of the lease or a catalogue item from the list, or type a new name; a new name can be saved to the catalogue for the next invoice. The description is an optional detail shown under the item. The line total is the quantity (empty: 1) times the unit amount, rounded to the cent.

Rent: choose Rent in the Item box, then the lease and the month; the unit amount is the month's rent (prorated for a partial first month, or the amount the rent run already charged) and stays editable; empty, it is that amount. The quantity is 1. Sending makes the month's rent charge, or takes the one the rent run made, so the month is billed once.

Existing open charge: the line bills that charge (for example the deposit of the setup checklist) instead of making a new one; the amount is its balance and cannot be changed here; to bill a different amount, edit the charge first.
