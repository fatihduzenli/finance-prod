{% comment %}The builder's Lines card (PLAN-012 D6; PLAN-012b D2; PLAN-012d D10; PLAN-012f-a D5, create-invoice-laptop.png): one Item box per line on one list that starts with Rent, then the lease's existing open charges, then the catalogue items; a Rent line's lease and month selects sit under its Item box (.rent-field), filled from the rent-amount endpoint by the script; the Save box under the Item box; then Description, Qty, Unit amount, Total (the row's line total, |line_total) and the trash. Without script the form shows its rows, every field and a Remove box per row; ledger/invoice_lines.js adds rows ("+ Add line"), removes a row with its trash button (DELETE set, the row hidden), keeps the line totals, the SUMMARY card's lines and the total live, shows the Save box only on a typed item, the lease and month only on a Rent line, and an existing charge's quantity and amount read-only. Each control has a visually hidden label naming its line and column (_line_label.html; the Save box's label names its line the same way). The table sits in the scroll region (PLAN-012e-3 D5), so a wide table scrolls inside it and the page stays within the viewport. The memo and the due date are in the SUMMARY card (_summary.html).{% endcomment %} {% load responsive money builder icons %}
