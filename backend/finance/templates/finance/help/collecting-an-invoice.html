{% comment %}How an invoice is collected: the five choices of the invoice builder's "How to collect", one sentence each (PLAN-012e-2 D3, D10; Set up autopay, PLAN-012f-a D12; PLAN-012f-b D15: Save and its e-mail prompt in place of Save draft and Send by e-mail, a choice not on offer greyed with its reason), and the line order of the invoice page.{% endcomment %}
Save
Saves the invoice as a draft, then asks "Send it to the operator by e-mail now?": Send e-mail creates the invoice in {{ company.provider_name }} and e-mails it to the operator, who pays it from the e-mail; Not now keeps the draft under Drafts on the store's Invoices tab, where Send by e-mail sends it later. Until it is sent, nothing reaches {{ company.provider_name }} and no one is charged.
Charge now
Debits the operator's linked bank account at once, after a confirmation page (admin only).
Schedule for a date
Debits the operator's linked bank account once, on a date after today and at most {{ schedule_horizon_days }} days ahead, for an active lease and an invoice of one item line.
Set up autopay
Repeats this invoice's one line as bank debits of the operator's linked bank account, monthly, weekly or yearly from a first date, ending after a number of payments, on a date or never. The plan appears on the store's Autopay tab, where it can be paused, resumed or stopped. Rent is never set up this way.
Record payment now
Records a wire, Zelle or other payment already received: the invoice is created in {{ company.provider_name }} and marked paid there, never e-mailed.

A choice that cannot be used is greyed with one line saying why: Charge now needs an admin and bank debits on at {{ company.provider_name }}; Charge now, Schedule for a date and Set up autopay need a bank account on file.

A payment on this invoice pays the lines in order; a refund or a return takes it back from the last line first.
