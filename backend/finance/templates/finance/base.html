{% comment %} Layout of the staff screens (docs/qa/PLAN-006-operators.md; the shell of docs/qa/PLAN-012f-approved-design.md D3, D4 and docs/design/DESIGN-SPEC.md §2, §6). Self-contained: no external asset; the style sheets (tokens.css with the self-hosted Inter, then app.css), the manifest, the icons and the service worker are the app's own (PLAN-011 D9, PLAN-012c D4, PLAN-012f D2). Laptop: the 232 px sidebar (#sidebar) holds the logo (company.logo_url: the brand's transparent dark logo, or a stored document the company settings row names, with the company name as text beside it) with the company name as its text alternative, the navigation (Dashboard, Stores, Operators, Monthly rent, Reports and Admin, each with its icon; Reports and Admin list their pages in a nested list open under the active group, each page only for the roles that may open it: Disputes, Daily charges and Nightly check billing and admin; Activity log and System settings admin; Messages from the processor every role, as its list is read-only) and the user card (#user-card: the name, the role, Settings, Help and Sign out, a POST form). The content column holds the page header (#page-header: {% block header %}, which a page fills with its .page-header (title and actions); the default is the page's title block as the heading; {% block header_search %} adds the search box) and the page (main, 28/32 px padding, at most 1,240 px wide). The current page's link carries aria-current="page" and its group's heading aria-current="true" (finance.context_processors.navigation). Phone (768 px and under): the top bar (#phone-bar: the menu button, a label of the #nav-toggle checkbox that opens the sidebar as a sheet; the logo; the search button, a label of #search-toggle that opens #phone-search), and the bottom tab bar (#tab-bar: Dashboard, Stores, Monthly rent and More, a
sheet with Operators, Reports, the Admin pages the role may open, Settings, Help and Sign out as a POST form; the item that holds the page is marked). The sheets work without script; the script below closes an open one on a click outside it and on Escape. The tab title is " · " (finance.context_processors.company): each page's title block holds the page part only. Flash messages are .toast items (PLAN-012c D5): the level's icon (.toast-icon: check, alert, dot, info; PLAN-012f-b E-4), the text, a dismiss button (the script below removes the message), a CSS fade (opacity) after 8 s for success and information, the text kept in the page. print.css (media="print") drops the sidebar, the phone bars and the messages and shows the print header (the company name and the date) and footer ("Printed ") (D6). The SANDBOX banner and the title prefix are injected by finance.middleware.SandboxBannerMiddleware; app.css places the banner above the page header. finance/dialog.js runs the {% dialog %} component (PLAN-012f-b D2) on every page. The dialog block, after the content, holds a confirmation page's open {% dialog %}: the confirmation template extends its parent page's template and fills only this block (and its title), so the parent page shows behind the dialog (PLAN-012f-b E-4). The sign-in pages keep their own base, two_factor/_base.html. The theme (PLAN-012g A2, A3; DESIGN-SPEC §7.1): is the user's stored choice (finance.context_processors.theme: light, dark or system; tokens.css resolves System through prefers-color-scheme, so no script and no flash); a page that must stay light (the invoice preview) overrides {% block theme %} and {% block theme_color %}. The theme-color meta follows it (finance/_theme_color.html).
