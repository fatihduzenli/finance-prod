{% comment %}Promises to pay: the paragraph the Add entry form lost (PLAN-012e-2 D10).{% endcomment %}

When the operator has an open promise to pay, the entry says what became of it. Still open records this entry and leaves the promise as it is; Kept closes it as kept; Rescheduled closes it and this entry is the new promise (its date and amount start from the old one); Broken closes it as broken.
